# Airline Ticket Cancellation Policy

## Overview
Flight bookings made via TravelAgent AI can be cancelled online or through our AI Assistant prior to scheduled flight departure. Cancellation fees depend on the airline fare rules and the time remaining before departure.

## Domestic Flight Cancellation Fee Tiers

### Cancellation More than 72 Hours Prior to Departure
- **Standard Cancellation Fee**: A flat airline cancellation fee of ₹1,500 per passenger per sector is deducted.
- The remaining balance of the base fare, fuel surcharges, and statutory government taxes are refunded to the original payment method.

### Cancellation Between 24 and 72 Hours Prior to Departure
- **Tier 2 Cancellation Fee**: A cancellation fee of ₹2,500 per passenger per sector (or the total base fare + fuel surcharge, whichever is lower) is deducted.
- Statutory airport taxes (UDF, PSF, and Goods & Services Tax) are 100% refundable.

### Cancellation Less than 24 Hours Prior to Departure
- **Short-Notice Cancellation**: The base fare and airline carrier charges are completely non-refundable.
- Government airport development fees (UDF) and passenger service fees (PSF) remain eligible for refund.

## 24-Hour Zero-Penalty Cancellation Grace Window
In accordance with DGCA civil aviation passenger charter:
- Any booking cancelled within **24 hours of initial ticket purchase** is eligible for a **100% full refund with zero cancellation charges**.
- **Condition**: The flight departure date must be at least **7 calendar days (168 hours)** away at the time of booking.

## Airline-Initiated Cancellations and Schedule Delays
If the airline cancels a flight or reschedules departure by more than 3 hours:
- **Full Refund**: Passengers are entitled to an immediate 100% refund without any cancellation deduction.
- **Alternative Option**: Free flight rescheduling to the next available partner flight on the same route.
