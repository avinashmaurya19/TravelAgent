# Airline Refund and Reimbursement Policy

## Refund Processing Channels and Timelines
All refunds are initiated deterministically through our automated booking settlement service.

### Processing Turnaround Time (TAT) by Payment Mode
- **UPI (PhonePe, Google Pay, Paytm)**: 24 to 48 business hours.
- **Net Banking**: 3 to 5 business days.
- **Credit Cards & Debit Cards**: 5 to 7 business days (depending on the issuing bank's settlement cycle).

## Refund Destination
- Refunds are credited strictly back to the **original source payment instrument or bank account** used at the time of booking.
- Third-party transfers or cash reimbursements are not permitted under AML compliance guidelines.

## Add-On Services and Ancillary Refunds
- **Seat Selection Fees**: Refundable if cancellation occurs at least 24 hours prior to scheduled departure.
- **Prepaid Extra Baggage**: 100% refundable if requested before flight departure.
- **Convenience Fees / Platform Fees**: Non-refundable under all regular passenger-initiated cancellation requests.

## Medical Emergencies and Bereavement Policy
- In the event of passenger hospitalization or direct family bereavement prior to travel date:
  - Airline may grant a special refund waiver or credit voucher upon submission of valid hospital admission certificates or medical documentation.
  - Processing requests require submission within 7 days of the missed flight.
