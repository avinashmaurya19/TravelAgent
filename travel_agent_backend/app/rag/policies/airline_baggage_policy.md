# Airline Baggage Allowance Policy

## Standard Cabin Baggage Policy
All domestic passengers are permitted to carry one piece of hand baggage with a maximum weight of 7 kg.
- Dimensions must not exceed 55 cm x 35 cm x 25 cm (total linear dimension 115 cm).
- In addition to standard hand baggage, one small personal item (such as a laptop bag, lady's purse, or infant duty item not exceeding 3 kg) is permitted free of charge.

## Domestic Check-in Baggage Allowances by Airline
Different partner airlines enforce distinct check-in baggage limits for Economy class:

### IndiGo (6E) Baggage Allowance
- **Economy Standard Allowance**: 15 kg per passenger (single piece).
- **Additional Allowance**: Pre-purchased prepaid baggage available in slabs of 5 kg, 10 kg, 15 kg, and 30 kg.

### Air India (AI) Baggage Allowance
- **Economy Class**: 20 kg to 25 kg per passenger (depending on fare family: Comfort Economy allows 20 kg, Flex Economy allows 25 kg).
- **Business Class**: 35 kg per passenger.
- **Infant Allowance**: 10 kg complimentary check-in baggage.

### Akasa Air (QP) Baggage Allowance
- **Standard Allowance**: 15 kg per passenger (maximum dimensions 158 cm linear).
- **Infant Allowance**: 7 kg check-in baggage complimentary.

### SpiceJet (SG) Baggage Allowance
- **Standard Allowance**: 15 kg per passenger.
- **SpicMax Premium**: 20 kg allowance included with priority baggage handling.

## Excess Baggage Charges
If your baggage exceeds the complimentary allowance:
- **Pre-Booked Rate**: ₹550 per additional kilogram when booked online up to 2 hours prior to departure.
- **Airport Counter Rate**: ₹600 per additional kilogram when paid at airport check-in counters.
- **Overweight Single Bag Policy**: No single piece of check-in luggage may exceed 32 kg in compliance with airport occupational safety standards.

## Special Baggage and Sports Equipment
- Musical instruments, golf kits, ski equipment, and bicycles require pre-registration.
- A special handling fee of ₹1,200 to ₹1,500 applies per item depending on dimensions.
